Privacy Policy: Run That Back

Effective date: November 10, 2026
Publisher: Draftworthy (Joel Lynch)

Run That Back is a reflex game. It does not collect your personal information.

What the game stores, and where

Your best scores, your Daily Challenge history, your settings, and any saved challenge results are stored only on your own device, in the game's local storage. They are never sent to Draftworthy or to anyone else. Deleting the game, or using the "clear saved scores" control inside it, removes them.

What the game does not do

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no names or email addresses are requested.
  • No analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access.
  • No data is sold, shared, or transmitted to third parties, because no data leaves your device.

Challenge links and codes

When you choose to share a run, the game creates a challenge link or code that encodes that run's sequence and your score, so a friend can play the identical run. Creating and sending that link is your action, through whatever app you choose to share it with; Draftworthy receives nothing from it.

Purchases

If you buy the optional Full Unlock, the transaction is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Draftworthy does not receive or store your payment details.

Children

The game is rated 4+ and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version ever changes what data the game handles, this policy will be updated and the change noted here before that version is released.
